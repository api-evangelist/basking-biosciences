## What this company does

Basking Biosciences develops BB-031, an investigational RNA aptamer targeting von Willebrand
Factor (vWF) as a rapid-onset, targeted, and reversible thrombolytic for acute ischemic stroke.
It is paired with BB-025, a direct-acting reversal agent designed to rapidly neutralize BB-031 —
the reversibility is the differentiator, since existing thrombolytics cannot be switched off if a
patient bleeds. The company is running the RAISE Phase 2 clinical trial and is backed by Insight
Partners.
